# AI Use Record

## Scope of AI assistance

AI assistance was used to structure the assessment, draft workflow logic, propose synthetic test scenarios, compare public job descriptions with visible course titles, and prepare a simple browser-based comparison tool. The author remains fully responsible for the final claims, source checks, calculations, and submission quality.

**Model recorded:** GPT-6  
**Date:** 23 October 2026

## Verification approach

The package follows a controlled verification model:

- Task 1 was limited to the scenario supplied in the prompt. Additional process details and all timing estimates are explicitly labelled assumptions.
- Course names and semester totals were transcribed from the supplied photograph and verified for arithmetic consistency. The visible totals sum to 162, while the printed grand total appears to be 163; this discrepancy is disclosed and not silently corrected.
- Public job pages were reviewed and recorded with access dates, skill requirements, and a note where the role clearly implies seniority or experience requirements.
- AI-assisted subject exercises are recorded in [task-2-curriculum-audit/ai-verification-log.csv](task-2-curriculum-audit/ai-verification-log.csv), with verification methods and human review noted.
- The interactive tool in [task-2-curriculum-audit/interactive-tool/index.html](task-2-curriculum-audit/interactive-tool/index.html) was designed for local demonstration and should be checked in a browser before final submission.

## Professional use policy

This is not a claim that AI independently verified the official curriculum, job suitability, or any institutional process. Any answer was treated as a draft input that required human review. No private academic data, student records, or confidential internal information was used.

## Limitations

The AI record is intentionally conservative. It records the output summary, the human verification method, and the relevance of the result without presenting conceptual reasoning as a production-system test. Where a check was conceptual rather than executable, that limitation is stated clearly.
