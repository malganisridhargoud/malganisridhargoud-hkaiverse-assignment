## If access becomes available before submission

Ask one willing faculty member or recent graduate, while avoiding personal or identifying details:

1. Which proposed change addresses a real learning gap, and which one appears redundant?
2. Is the proposed workflow-engineering lab feasible within the current prerequisites and credit structure?
3. What kind of evidence would most change your view of this proposal?

If feedback is collected, record only:

- the respondent's role,
- the question asked,
- a non-identifying summary of the response,
- the proposed change, if any, triggered by that feedback.

Respect any refusal and do not pressure a participant for a response.
