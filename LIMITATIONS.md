# Overall Limitations and Final Checks

This package is deliberately transparent about the limits of the evidence it uses.

1. Task 1 is hypothetical and based only on the scenario supplied in the brief; the draft does not claim direct observation or permissioned field study.
2. All volume, timing, and efficiency values in the Task 1 model are illustrative assumptions rather than measured institutional data.
3. The supplied curriculum photograph does not identify the exact admission batch, regulation version, or official document URL. The applicable official syllabus and regulation must be confirmed before any curriculum recommendation is treated as final.
4. The visible semester totals add to 162 credits, while the printed grand total appears to be 163. This discrepancy is preserved intentionally and not silently resolved.
5. Semester VII and VIII are shown only as bundled 15-credit totals; the individual course-level allocations are not legible and should not be invented.
6. Public job postings are time-sensitive and may include seniority requirements. They are used as skill signals, not as proof of fresher eligibility.
7. No faculty, graduate, or institutional feedback is claimed without a real voluntary response.
8. The proposed curriculum changes remain proposals and require curriculum-owner, programme, and regulatory approval before implementation.
9. The interactive calculator is a local demonstration only and is not a production student information system.
10. Before final submission, verify the interactive page, check all links, add the final GitHub repository/release/commit metadata, and retain the submission receipt.
