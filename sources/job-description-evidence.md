# Job Description Evidence Record

## Evidence policy

This record keeps only job postings that are public, directly accessible, and dated at the time of access. One requirement is that the posting must not be described as a fresher role when the page itself clearly signals seniority or experience requirements. The analysis uses these postings as a skills signal, not as proof of entry-level hiring or official programme equivalence.

## Summary of use in the curriculum audit

The package retains at least three dated public postings with direct links and concrete skill expectations. These are used to justify targeted assessment updates, not to claim that these jobs are entry-level roles or that the programme is already aligned by title alone. The evidence is strongest when the skill requirement is paired with a visible relevant course and a documented gap in practical assessment.
